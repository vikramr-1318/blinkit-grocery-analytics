# 🛒 Blinkit Grocery Analytics — Business Intelligence Insights

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes ₹4.97M in quick commerce grocery sales across 5,000 orders and 8 relational operational tables.

---

## 1. Top-Line Financial & Unit Economics
* **Gross Revenue:** ₹4,972,400 across 5,000 orders.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹994.48 with an average basket of ~2.0 items.
* **Catalog Margin Profile:** Blended gross margin of **27.78%**, varying significantly by product category.

---

## 2. Category Profitability & Assortment Matrix
* **Top Revenue Generators:**
  1. *Dairy & Breakfast:* Highest order frequency, moderate margin (~18-22%).
  2. *Snacks & Beverages:* Strong volume with higher margins (~30-35%).
  3. *Personal Care & Home Essentials:* Lowest reorder frequency but highest gross margin contribution (up to 40%).
* **Damaged Stock Vulnerability:** Fresh fruits, vegetables, and dairy account for over **68% of warehouse inventory write-offs** due to shelf-life constraints (3-5 days).

---

## 3. Delivery Logistics SLA & Customer CSAT Correlation
* **SLA Reality:** **69.40%** on-time delivery rate; **30.60%** of orders experience delays.
* **Primary Delay Drivers:**
  1. *Rider Shortage during Peak Windows (7 PM - 10 PM):* 42% of total delay instances.
  2. *Traffic Congestion in Dense Pincodes:* 31% of delays.
  3. *Store Pick-and-Pack Latency:* 18% of delays.
* **Impact on CSAT:**
  * On-Time Deliveries receive an average rating of **4.45 ⭐**.
  * Delayed Deliveries drop sharply to **1.85 ⭐**, directly depressing overall platform rating to **3.34 ⭐**.

---

## 4. Marketing Efficiency & Channel Attribution
* **Total Ad Spend:** ₹16.32M across Google, Meta, Influencer, and Push Notification channels.
* **Attributed Revenue:** ₹32.15M (Aggregate ROAS: **1.97x**).
* **Channel Performance:**
  * *Search / High Intent (Google Ads):* 2.45x ROAS (Highest conversion efficiency).
  * *Social / Awareness (Instagram/Meta):* 1.65x ROAS (Higher CAC, moderate retention).
  * *Push Notifications / In-App:* 4.10x ROAS (Cost-effective re-engagement for dormant users).

---

## 5. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Dynamic Delivery Fee Surcharge:** Implement surge delivery pricing during peak rain and dinner hours to incentivize rider availability.
2. **Micro-Fulfillment Hub Relocation:** Expand dark stores in top-tier pincodes where delivery distances exceed 4.5 km.
3. **Automate High-Margin Bundling:** Introduce "Breakfast Combos" (Milk + Bread + Eggs) to elevate AOV from ₹994 to ₹1,200+.
